Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024

Welcome to SVS Residency! Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our resident portal and mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to provide and manage our society services:

  • Personal Details: Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Date of Birth, Gender.
  • Residency Details: Flat Number, Occupancy Status (Owner/Tenant), Vehicle Numbers.
  • Technical Data: Firebase Authentication identifiers, device access logs for session management.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for community management and security purposes:

  • To verify your identity and approve access to the society portal.
  • To communicate society announcements, notices, and dues statements.
  • To manage facility bookings and resolve complaints.
  • To enhance community security by tracking approved residents and vehicles.

3. Data Protection and Security

We use industry-standard security measures (including Google Firebase) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access. We do not sell or share your personal data with third-party marketing agencies.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data as long as you are an active resident or owner. If you vacate the premises or delete your account, your personal data will be removed from active databases in accordance with our administrative policies.

5. Your Rights

As a user, you have the right to view, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data by contacting the SVS Residency administration team through the portal's Account Deletion request feature.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any major changes via the Notice Board feature in the application.
