Help & Support

Find answers to common questions and get support for using SVS Residency.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download the SVS Residency app?

SVS Residency is available on the Google Play Store. Search for "SVS Residency" or visit the link from our website. For iOS support, please contact us at the email provided below.

Is my personal data secure?

Yes! We use enterprise-grade encryption and security measures to protect your personal information. Please review our Privacy Policy for detailed information about how we handle your data.

How do I view my maintenance billing information?

Open the app and navigate to the "Digital Accounting" section. You can view all receipts, outstanding balances, and download financial statements. The information is updated in real-time by the society management committee.

Can I change the language of the app?

Absolutely! SVS Residency supports Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Malayalam, and Kannada. Go to Settings → Language to select your preferred language. The app will display all content, including notices and communications, in your chosen language with AI-powered translation.

How do I book a facility like the Cellular?

Navigate to the "Facility Booking" section in the app. You'll see available amenities with real-time availability calendars. Select your preferred date and time, and your booking will be confirmed immediately with a notification to the management committee.

What should I do if I encounter a bug or technical issue?

Please contact us using the form on our Contact page or email us directly at remgeorge@gmail.com. Describe the issue in detail, including your device model and app version, and we'll respond promptly.

How do I submit a complaint or feedback?

We value your feedback! Use the in-app feedback feature or contact us through our Contact page. Your suggestions help us improve the platform for everyone.

Is there a cost to use SVS Residency?

SVS Residency is provided as a service to your society. Pricing details and subscription information should be discussed with your society management committee.

What do I do if I forget my password?

On the login screen, click "Forgot Password?" and follow the instructions. You'll receive an email with a password reset link. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

How do I disable notifications?

Go to Settings → Notifications and toggle off the types of notifications you don't want to receive. You can customize alerts for notices, bookings, maintenance updates, and community messages.

Still Need Help?

If you couldn't find the answer to your question, please don't hesitate to contact us. Our support team is here to help!
