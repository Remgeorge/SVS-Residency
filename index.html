Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 07, 2026

Welcome to SVS Residency. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, share, and safeguard information when you use our application and services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information such as your name, contact details, apartment or resident information, maintenance-related records, and communications you send through the platform.

How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us provide resident services, process maintenance requests, send notices, manage community communication, and improve the overall experience for the society.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with authorized society management personnel, service providers assisting us with operations, or as required by law.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Your Choices

You may contact us to review, update, or request details about the personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law and verification requirements.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact the SVS Residency management team through the contact details provided on this website.
